## Advisor notes (what the proposal misses)

- **A1 Continuity is a missing concept, not a missing screen.** No model in the repo represents "who covers this". Proposed minimal model: `Coverage { tenantId, subjectType, subjectId, primaryPartyId, backupPartyId, validFrom, validTo, reviewedAt, reviewedById }`, applied to Position, Project, Cohort, Account, Lead, HrCase. Controls: assertCan on coverage edit (not self), auditWrite (register `coverage`), emit `kz.hr.coverage.assigned`, tenant scope on every query. Uses: exit gate (no handover done without a backup), leave approval fallback, trainer substitution. Dependencies: Phase 1 is not required; uses Assignment and Affiliation. Size M.
- **A2 Handover as a gate, not a checklist.** Structured HandoverItem `{ kind: customer_case | teaching | project | mailbox | number | vendor | knowledge | asset, fromEmploymentId, toPartyId, acknowledgedById, acknowledgedAt, status }`. Offboarding moves to ClearancePending only when all items are acknowledged by receivers. The proposal's "receiving owners acknowledge" is this record. Size M.
- **A3 Exit has no retention split.** Employment history must be kept by schedule while personal data is minimised on request. The proposal says "retained according to the approved schedule" without naming the schedule owner. Needs RetentionSchedule rules (exists, `compliance-privacy.prisma:176`) per record class. Statutory retention periods UNVERIFIED here; do not hardcode (CLAUDE rule 4).
- **A4 Leave reason sensitivity.** LeaveRequest.reason is free text (`main.prisma:4488-4505`). The proposal's "no private medical reasons" needs a sensitivity class on the reason, and approvers should see a category, not text, unless their grant allows. Team calendar is already safe (`leavepolicy.ts:905-930`).
- **A5 Statutory uncertainty flagged, not decided.** (a) Gratuity: the formula and eligibility in code (`packages/shared/src/compliance/payroll.ts:221-234`) and the wage definition for gratuity are contested in the labour-code transition; this review does not determine the current rule, so the parameters must come from StatutoryRule with a source (F5). (b) The plan states the Income-tax Act 2025 took effect 1 April 2026 (`docs/plan/phase-3-statutory.md:35`); not verified here. (c) PF and ESI ceilings and rates are data in tables (`compliance-payroll.prisma:13-82`); their sources are not recorded in the schema. Stop and flag before any constant changes.
- **A6 Bank rules are outside code and must be sourced, not invented.** Beneficiary verification channel, cutoff days, NEFT or RBI obligations, and bank return codes are operational and regulatory facts to be confirmed with the bank partner and counsel. The design (versioned beneficiary, two-person segregation, freeze per batch) is safe to build; the specific cutoff and verification rules are not. Flagged as statutory and operational uncertainty.
- **A7 Salary-account referral.** The proposal's position (Zen 360 is not a bank; referral only, bank performs KYC) is correct and matches the code: no account-opening, no credentials, no sweep (none found; grep for sweep, OTP, login: none). Keep it as a referral record with consent. Size S.

- **A9 Education fees have existing tax machinery.** CourseGstExemption `compliance-gst.prisma:15` and `gst.ts:51-59` (courseGstExemptionFor) exist. The proposal does not mention it. Use it for concessions and refunds, not new tax logic.
- **A10 Concessions and refund entitlements are missing from the proposal.** Refund machinery is generic (refunds.ts:34-70, invoice or receipt only). A learner-level entitlement needs: agreement reference, entitlement basis (instructor interruption, missed sessions), calculation in paise (F1), approval by finance, and a link to the learner support case.

- **A12 Recording and transcripts.** Do not build capture first. Child or learner consent, notice, retention, provider capability and restricted access are prerequisites. A transcript is evidence of speech, not attendance; enforce that in code (item 97).
