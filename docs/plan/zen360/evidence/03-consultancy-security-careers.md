## Advisor notes (what the proposal misses)

Missing from the proposal entirely:

- **Conflict of interest across clients.** Consultancy and security both require it before access. Also matters for audit firms that later serve competitors of an existing client.
- **Professional liability and insurance.** Engagement-level liability cap, insurance certificate, and which package carries it. The proposal says "each package retains its own scope and liability" (p13) but no record holds that.
- **Client data processing terms.** For Careers and Security, Kaizen holds personal data of a client's staff or candidates. Processor vs fiduciary role under DPDP is not modelled. UNVERIFIED how the Act applies to each role; counsel.
- **Authority to test.** Written authorisation is the legal basis for authorised testing. Without it, testing activity may fall under the IT Act 2000 offences. UNVERIFIED exact sections; counsel to confirm before any testing is offered.
- **Qualification register.** The proposal says "sell only services with qualified delivery" (p13) but there is no model that defines qualified. Needed before any quote.
- **Independence of reviewers.** Peer review requires the reviewer not to be on the engagement team or have a conflict.
- **Benefit confirmation.** "Independent confirmation" (p13) needs a named confirmer who is not the sales owner. Not specified.
- **Engagement-level budget control.** Scope creep is listed as an exception but no threshold or change-order trigger is given.
- **Statutory mapping for workforce models.** The proposal treats apprentices, interns and third-party employees as service options without the statutory layer. Labour Codes are in force from 21 November 2025 (`docs/plan/phase-3-statutory.md:33`); mapping to them (employer definitions, contract-labour registration, apprenticeship rules) is a Phase 3 item and must not be guessed. Tamil Nadu rules status per the plan is still to verify.
- **Placement fee tax.** GST treatment of placement and manpower-supply fees: rate and SAC must be StatutoryRule rows (R4). UNVERIFIED which SAC applies.
- **Incident disclosure timing.** Proposal says "approved disclosure route" (p14) without a legal deadline. Indian CERT-In directions are reported to require incident reporting within a short window; UNVERIFIED, must be confirmed and configured as a dated rule, not a constant.
- **Retention and deletion for CCTV and evidence.** Proposal says retention controls exist but gives no default or legal basis.
- **Cross-tenant evidence.** For a holding with several operating units, evidence for a client must not reach other units. Group snapshot logic (`group.ts:203`) has no sensitivity filter.
- **Client-side portal.** Restricted cases, finding acceptance and benefit sign-off are client actions. No client identity or external-user model is in the proposal or in the permissions model (`packages/shared/src/permissions.ts`). This is a larger gap than it looks.
- **Acceptance tests are good.** The proposal's acceptance paragraphs (p13 close, p14 denied out-of-scope test, p15 student-to-candidate-to-client join) are testable. Adopt them verbatim as test cases.

Ambiguities in the proposal:

- "Digital may own software, Education learning, Careers staffing and Security a risk review" (p13). Security is described both as a client-facing service (p14) and as Kaizen's own IT risk review (matches `ItSecurityFinding`, built for Kaizen's own IT per `docs/plan/cio.md:362-370`). These are different records with different access rules. The proposal does not separate them.
- "Security practice" and "managed security" both sit under "Security service archetype". Managed security implies 24x7 operations and on-call staffing; the proposal names it but does not size it.
- "Authorised live CCTV viewing" (p14) is a physical-security product inside an ERP. It is not connected to any other part of the proposal, and the proposal's own "do not infer identity" rule presupposes a capability the platform lacks. Recommend deferral or removal.
- Careers "Kaizen-employed deployment" implies Kaizen is the legal employer of deployed workers, with full statutory duties. The proposal does not say whether this model will be offered at all.
- "Success fees require a validated definition" (p13) — who validates, and against what?
- "Recruitment-only: candidate joins the client; no automatic Kaizen employment or payroll" (p15). The current code always creates Kaizen employment on join (`hiring.ts:291-296`). The proposal implies a model that the code cannot produce.
- Citation tags [B5], [B6], [B9], [B12], [B16], [W10] are not in these pages; not verified here.

## Decisions for leadership

1. **Practice placement.** Run consultancy, security and careers inside the current tenant (requires the `Project` collision fix and a practice discriminator) or as separate tenants or entities (requires the multi-entity decision in `docs/plan/README.md:39`). Recommend deciding before any engagement model is built.
2. **Does the platform hold client restricted evidence at all?** If yes: separate secure store, legal review, and a retention policy per client. If no: security practice runs its evidence outside the platform and the platform holds only the finding summary.
3. **CCTV: go or no-go.** No code, no plan, no owner. Recommend removing from this proposal or moving to a partner integration.
4. **Which Careers service models launch first.** Recommend recruitment-only and payroll-only last, because they need the service-model change first (`hiring.ts:291-296`). Kaizen-employed deployment has the largest statutory exposure as the employer of record.
5. **Placement fee terms.** Whether guarantees and refunds are offered. If yes, the refund liability must be a ledger item (R2) with an approval path; the proposal does not say who approves a guarantee.
6. **Success fees.** Offer or not. If offered, who confirms the benefit and what accounting standard applies (UNVERIFIED for the entity).
7. **Risk-acceptance authority.** Who may accept a security risk for a client, and whether the client must countersign. Current code allows any `edit` holder (`findings.ts:166`).
8. **Qualification register owner.** Who maintains the list of qualified delivery staff, and what evidence is needed. Without this, "sell only qualified services" cannot be enforced.
9. **Client portal.** Whether clients get logins to accept findings, sign off benefits or view deployments. This is a new identity surface with its own permission model.
10. **Sequencing.** Practice work depends on Phase 1 (ledger and paise, R1/R2), Phase 2 (consent, DPDP), Phase 3 (statutory rules for GST and labour), and HCM position model. Leadership should accept that none of the practice features can ship before Phase 1 and Phase 2 complete.
