## Headline verdicts

1. **There are zero model calls in the repo.** No provider SDK in any `package.json`, no `@anthropic-ai`/`openai` import, no `fetch` to a model endpoint. `ModelGateway.call()` (`apps/api/src/platform/llm.ts:220-230`) only sanitises a payload and returns it. Nothing outside `llm.ts` and tests imports the gateway. The README's "An AI-native, multi-tenant ERP" (`README.md:3`) is not supported by code. The marketing "AI" (`apps/api/src/domains/marketing/ai.ts:205-218`) is a deterministic draft that registers an AgentAction.
2. **The AI policy fence is weaker than its comments say, and two of its approval controls are unguarded routes.** Details in Fixes F1–F5.
3. **The semantic layer is a compiler with no permission enforcement and no eval in CI.** `packages/semantic` has 2 golden cases (`packages/semantic/src/evals/golden.ts:11-35`), `runGoldenEval` is never called (`packages/semantic/src/eval.ts:46`), and `.github/workflows/ci.yml` contains no eval step. A filter operator is interpolated into SQL unvalidated (`packages/semantic/src/compiler.ts:133`).
4. **The event bus is tamper-evident in name only.** `verifyChain` never recomputes a hash and checks only the first 5,000 rows, yet reports `valid: true` (`apps/api/src/platform/eventBus.ts:422-450`). This matters because the proposal's traceability and PROV claims rest on the chain.
5. **Cross-domain handoffs are almost entirely unlinked.** Roughly 20 subscriptions exist (`apps/api/src/events/handlers.ts:72,112,149,154,161,165,174,193,207,215,218,223,248,253,260`; `apps/api/src/domains/hcm/leavepolicy.ts:970`), and most are projections or logs. No outbox exists, and dead letters cannot be replayed (`apps/api/src/routes/admin.routes.ts:272-281` lists only).

## Advisor notes (what the proposal misses)

1. **It does not say AI is not built.** The proposal (p28) calls for evaluation and oversight of AI features, but it never states that zero model calls exist and that every AI tier is a policy label. Leadership should read the AI section as a future commitment, not a description of the platform.
2. **Autonomous tiers that touch money and accounting are already in the policy file.** AI-COML-001 (revenue treatment, `ai.ts:238`), AI-LEAD-001, AI-ROUTE-001, AI-XCP-001, AI-MKT-006 and AI-ORG-001 are AUTONOMOUS. Combined with F1 and F5, the enforcement is wrong. The proposal does not mention this.
3. **Event integrity claim is not yet defensible.** Any claim of "tamper-evident organisational memory" (p27 and PROV framing) requires F8 and F9 first. Otherwise the chain is an append log with an unsound verifier.
4. **Preconditions for analytics are not stated.** OCEL, bottleneck analysis and prediction (p27) all need event completeness. Today events exist for ~20 subscriptions and 516 names, but most domain writes are not paired with an event (e.g. the leave transaction path `leave.ts:357-406` emits after commit with no outbox). Propose a completeness metric per domain before any analytics.
5. **Consent is written but not checked.** The proposal's Careers consent (p24) and DPDP-style purpose limitation depend on ConsentLedger reads, which do not exist (see p24 row). The region rule is wired: `preparePayload` calls `assertTenantRegion()` (`llm.ts:199`), which reads only the tenant's configured region (`llm.ts:103-116`). It does not check where a provider actually processes data, because no provider exists yet. Re-test when an adapter is added.
6. **Tamil and English evaluation.** The proposal lists Tamil/English examples (p28). No language field, transliteration or evaluation exists. This is a real requirement for Tamil Nadu operations, not a nice-to-have.
7. **Provider failure and cost.** The proposal names provider failures and cost ceilings (p28). The gateway defines the knobs (F21) but none is enforced, and there is no provider adapter to fail.
8. **Vacancy and capacity.** Workday-style vacant ownership (p25) and shared-specialist conflicts (p24) both reduce to one missing structure: a dated allocation or position-incumbency record. The proposal treats them separately; they share one data model.
9. **"Organisational intelligence" versus "digital twin".** The proposal correctly forbids a model score as a twin (p27). Its own list then asks for Scenario simulation (p27) without an accountable owner for the assumptions. Name the owner.
10. **Group identity model is undecided.** Legal entity is a tenant (`main.prisma:52`) and also a LedgerEntity inside a tenant (`compliance-books.prisma:54`). Restructuring, multi-role persons and recharges all depend on which one is canonical. The proposal does not choose.

12. **Restricted-fund and grant accounting** (p24, p26 Bridgespan, Salesforce grantmaking): the proposal says "validated purpose and authority" but does not say who validates, against which instrument, or which accounting treatment. These are accountant and counsel questions (UNVERIFIED here).
