## Direction contract

THESIS: Depth carries hierarchy instead of border weight — the refused
default is "flat because it's an ERP," the assumption that density and
softness can't coexist. Standing-exit/canon path (user-pinned, no seed
roll): Apple's system apps / macOS vibrancy, fused with Notion/Craft's
calm content-first cards, played straight at their fidelity.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground and ink text scale stay (contrast, division and
status colors are data, not aesthetic — untouched). Hierarchy moves to a
three-step elevation system: resting (flat, on-ground), raised (soft
dual-layer shadow, 1px hairline, translucent surface), floating (stronger
shadow, glass/backdrop-blur chrome for sidebar, modals, sticky headers).
Corners open further (16–28px). Headings drop uppercase/heavy-tracking for
macOS/Notion-style size+weight hierarchy; grouped-list-style small-caps
labels (kicker/section labels) stay, since Apple's own Settings uses them.
Colored border-left KPI rails retire in favor of a tinted soft badge/icon.

STORY: A counsellor, an ops head or the Chairman should feel like they're
in a considered Apple-made tool doing serious bookkeeping — not a spreadsheet
wearing a dark sidebar. Depth clarifies which surface is active without
shouting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App shell — glass sidebar (blurred dark panel, gold active
pill unchanged), paper canvas, cards elevated with soft shadow + hairline,
KPI tiles as soft rounded tiles with a tinted icon chip replacing the color
rail, tables kept flat/on-ground (data itself, inside a raised card) with
crisp tabular numerals preserved.

FORM: Canon (standing exit) — Apple system apps/macOS + Notion/Craft,
confirmed by the user; no concept-seed roll, per new-work.md's rule that a
user-pinned direction beats the roll. Honest risk: glass/blur chrome must
not blur or reduce contrast of financial figures; depth is uniform
including the densest screens, per the user's explicit choice.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying
its provenance.
